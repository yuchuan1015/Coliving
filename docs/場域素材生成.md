## 完整提示詞組裝方式

每次提示詞依序串接「共用開頭」＋對應圖片段落＋「共用結尾」，如下。

### 共用開頭

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: original cinematic sci-fi architectural in-page background for a Coliving website; image asset only.
```

### orbital-garden.png

```text
Primary request: glass-domed space station botanical garden with living plants and quiet water; outer space and a planet visible through the glass.
Lighting/mood: tranquil, warm restrained gold lighting and deep plum navy shadows.
Materials/textures: realistic glass, architectural framing, naturally detailed leaves and softly reflective water.
```

### orbital-gallery.png

```text
Primary request: monumental orbital art museum with a display sculpture and framed original abstract art, a large planet visible through the window.
Lighting/mood: soft ivory lighting, contemplative cinematic atmosphere.
Color palette: deep purple and navy with muted gold.
Materials/textures: realistic stone with subtle grain, brushed metal and glass.
```

### orbital-lounge.png

```text
Primary request: intimate orbital communal lounge with round tables, board games and books, and a panoramic space window.
Lighting/mood: quiet water-like light, blue-violet shadows and restrained pools of warm amber light.
Materials/textures: realistic architecture and furniture, tactile book covers with no lettering, unlettered board game pieces.
```

### 共用結尾

```text
Style/medium: photorealistic cinematic architectural photography, believable construction, tactile real materials and physically plausible reflections.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9, wide-angle architectural view, balanced spacious composition suitable for a background.
Constraints: generate exactly one original image; no people, text, lettering, signage, UI, logos or watermark; no cartoon style or neon.
```
